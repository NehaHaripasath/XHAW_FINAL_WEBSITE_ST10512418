About us

Our history

Pawsitive Pet Academy was established in 2023 by Sarah Smith and offers training courses in Durban. Hundreds of pet owners and aspiring pet care professionals have completed our six-month Professional Development Programmes and six-week Practical Skills Courses.

Sarah noticed that many people own pets without knowing much about proper care, training, nutrition or emergency care. The academy exists to change that, and to create jobs in the pet care industry.

Vision

A community where every pet is cared for by someone who knows how.

Mission

To give people practical, professional pet care skills that improve animal wellbeing and open career opportunities.

Goals

  • Grow graduate numbers every year
  • Help graduates start or join pet businesses
  • Keep courses practical and affordable
